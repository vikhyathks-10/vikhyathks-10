## 👨‍💻 About Me

Hi, I'm **Vikhyath Bharadwaj K S**, a Computer Science and Engineering student at **PES University, Bengaluru**, with a strong interest in technology, problem-solving, and building practical solutions to real-world challenges.

My interests span **Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Analytics, and Software Development**. I enjoy exploring new ideas, understanding how systems work, and transforming concepts into meaningful projects. I believe technology is most valuable when it solves real problems through thoughtful design, efficient implementation, and continuous improvement.

Through my academic and personal projects, I have gained experience working with Python, C, C++, JavaScript, React, SQL, and Git/GitHub. My project work includes machine learning-based tennis match prediction, smart agricultural advisory systems, municipal solid waste route optimization, and privacy-focused research into federated learning for multi-cloud intrusion detection.

My involvement in college activities, including **Kannada Koota at PES University**, has also given me opportunities to contribute to content creation, event promotion, and team-based initiatives.

I am continuously strengthening my technical foundation, practising Data Structures and Algorithms, and exploring emerging technologies to expand my capabilities. I value curiosity, adaptability, and a hands-on approach to learning, and I aim to contribute to projects that combine technical innovation with practical impact.

My goal is to grow into a technology professional who can connect ideas, analytical thinking, and engineering to build useful, user-focused solutions.
